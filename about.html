ABOUT TELANGANA HOUSING BOARD

Telangana Housing Board (THB) is a Government organization working to provide affordable housing to the citizens of Telangana.


The Board develops residential layouts, housing colonies, commercial complexes, integrated townships and modern infrastructure across the state.


Its objective is to ensure planned urban development, provide quality housing and improve the standard of living for economically weaker sections, middle-income groups and other eligible beneficiaries.


Vision

To make quality housing accessible to every eligible family in Telangana.


Mission

To develop sustainable housing projects using modern technology, efficient planning and transparent administration.
